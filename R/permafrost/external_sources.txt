The supplied TIFFs are compact clips of the original products. Their native grids
and values are preserved; no value reclassification was applied. The comparison
extent is the Coffee training-map bounding box plus 5 km in EPSG:3579, recorded
in comparison_extent.csv. prepare_external_comparison() recreates these clips
when given the original TIFFs. The plotting function reprojects the clips for
display at 30 m and 1000 m respectively with nearest-neighbour interpolation.
The external products show permafrost occurrence probability. The field and random
forest maps show ground ice classes. This is a qualitative comparison, not an
accuracy benchmark or conversion between those different quantities.
